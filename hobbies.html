Hobbies & interests

When I am away from lectures, labs and assignment deadlines, these are the activities that keep me curious, healthy and creative. The first list shows my main hobbies in order of priority; the second shows the other things I enjoy.

I My main hobbies, in order

Ordered list — most important first

  1. Programming and web development — building small websites and practising new code between classes.
  2. Reading — technology articles, biographies and inspirational non-fiction.
  3. Football — playing with friends at the university grounds and following local and international leagues.
  4. Listening to music — especially Kenyan and East African artists.
  5. Volunteering and community service — helping with school and church activities.

II More things I enjoy

Unordered list — no particular ranking

  • Travelling to new places and learning about different cultures.
  • Photography — capturing everyday life and scenic landscapes.
  • Cooking simple meals and trying out new recipes.
  • Watching documentaries on science, technology and history.
  • Bicycle rides and keeping fit.
  • Chess and puzzle games that stretch my thinking.
  • Hiking — exploring the hills and trails around Kisii.

These hobbies are not just a break from study — they keep me balanced and sharpen the creativity and discipline I bring to my Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) at Kisii University.
